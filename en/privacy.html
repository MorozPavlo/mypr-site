Privacy Policy

Effective 10 October 2026

In short: MyPR collects no personal data, stores nothing on servers, and transmits nothing. Everything you enter stays only on your device.

What data we collect

None. MyPR has no servers of its own and does not send your data to us or any third party. We use no analytics, ad networks, trackers or third-party data-collection SDKs.

Data you enter in the app

The information you provide (age, education, work experience, language test scores, family details, etc.), along with assessment results and search history, is stored only locally on your device. This data never leaves the device and is accessible only to you.

On-device processing

Eligibility assessment runs entirely on the device using Apple's built-in, on-device artificial-intelligence capabilities. Your profile is not sent to the internet for analysis. The app works without a network connection.

Translation

Translating saved results uses Apple's on-device translation technology. Text processing is governed by Apple's privacy policy; MyPR does not receive or keep copies of this data on any server.

Third-party access

Because we collect no data, we sell, share and disclose nothing to third parties. Downloading and updating the app happens through the App Store and is governed by Apple's privacy policy.

Children

MyPR is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any data about children.

Deleting your data

You can delete saved profiles and history inside the app at any time. Deleting the app permanently removes all of its associated data from your device.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version is always available on this page, with its effective date.
