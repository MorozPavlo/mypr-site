Frequently asked questions

Is MyPR an official application channel?

No. MyPR is an informational self-assessment tool. It is not affiliated with the Government of Canada or Quebec and does not submit any applications on your behalf.

Is my data sent anywhere?

No. All analysis runs on your device. The app has no server and never sends your answers, profile or results to the internet. MyPR works even without a network connection.

How accurate are the results?

The assessment is based on published program criteria and is meant as a rough self-check. Criteria change and every case is unique — always verify with official sources (canada.ca, Quebec's MIFI website, provincial program pages) or a licensed immigration consultant.

The program data looks out of date — what do I do?

Program criteria are built into the app and refresh with each new version. Make sure you have the latest version from the App Store. The date the data was last updated is shown in Settings.

How do I delete my data?

All data is stored only on your device. Delete saved profiles and history inside the app, or delete the app — and all its data is gone with it.
